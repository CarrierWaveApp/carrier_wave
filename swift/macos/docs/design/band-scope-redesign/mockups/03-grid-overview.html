A 4 × 3 grid of small per-band cells covering all 11 HF bands plus 6 m. Each cell shows a tiny spot density histogram, mult / new / total counts, and a "heat" strip for activity over the last 5 minutes. Click a cell to expand it into the main scope (right side). The grid is for scanning, the expanded view is for acting.

A Every HF band visible at once. The only layout where the operator can answer "is there anything on 6 m?" or "did 80 m wake up?" without changing band. Each cell is a self-contained summary: spot count, mult count, density histogram, recent activity heat strip, age of last spot. B Drill-down via expansion. Click any cell to expand it into the right-side scope (which is the same single-band layout from variant 01). The grid stays visible. Acting happens in the expanded view, scanning happens in the grid. C Dead bands fade to 50% opacity. 6 m and 10 m at the bottom of the screenshot are visually muted so the eye goes straight to the bands with activity (40 / 20 / 15 m). D "All HF" summary cell. The 12th grid cell aggregates everything for a glance-level "is the contest hot right now?" check. Useful for the multi-op coordinator.
! Tradeoffs. Lowest interaction density per band — you can't do real S&P from the grid view alone. The grid is only useful if it's paired with the expanded view, which means the layout commits 580 + 812 = 1392 pt to itself. On a laptop screen this leaves no room for the QSO log, the entry field, or any other panel. Best as a full-window mode, not a permanent layout.
