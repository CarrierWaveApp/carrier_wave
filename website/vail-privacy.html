Privacy Policy

Vail Morse — Last updated: September 10, 2026

What Vail Morse Does

Vail Morse is an iOS client for the public Vail Morse code network. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Short Version

Vail Morse does not collect personal information. It does not include analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking. No account is required. The only data that leaves your device is what you actively transmit to the Vail network: your callsign, your chosen TX tone, the timing of your key transmissions, and any text chat messages you send. That data is relayed by the third-party server at vailmorse.com to other operators on the same channel, exactly as you would expect from a public chat or repeater.

Data We Collect

None. Vail Morse stores no personal data on any server we operate, does not create user accounts, and does not log usage information about you.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following are stored locally on your device only and never transmitted anywhere by the app:

  • Your chosen callsign and TX tone
  • Your channel history and last-used channel
  • Your contacts (callsign, name, notes, favorite status, last-worked dates)
  • Your scheduled meetups ("skeds")
  • Your audio, keyer, and interface preferences

These remain on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Data Transmitted to the Vail Network

When you connect to a Vail channel, your callsign, TX tone, key timing, and chat messages are sent to the third-party server at vailmorse.com and relayed to every operator on the same channel. Treat anything you send as public, just like a transmission on an open repeater. The vailmorse.com service is operated by a third party and has its own practices; the app only sends it the data described here.

Children

Vail Morse channels are open public chat. The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
