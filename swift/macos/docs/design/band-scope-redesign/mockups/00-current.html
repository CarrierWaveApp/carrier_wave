1 Tooltip pinned to the right edge. Hover a spot in the middle of the canvas, then jump your eye 800 pt to the right to read it. Should follow the cursor. 2 VFO frequency label overlaps the axis ticks. drawVFOCursor draws the red 14130.0 label trailing-anchored at plotX − 4, directly on top of the gray axis labels at zoomed-out scales. 3 Sub-band shading is invisible. The CW/digital and SSB sub-band hints are drawn at 4% opacity. On a typical macOS display you can't see them at all. 4 Zoom indicator is a tiny "2.0x" in the corner. No mini-map, no overview, no sense of where in the band the visible region is. 5 Cross-band activity is buried in capsule chips. The single most strategic question — "should I QSY?" — is answered by chips ≤ 14 pt tall in a footer the eye filters out. 6 No mini-map / overview. Once you zoom, you lose your sense of where you are in the band. There is no thumbnail strip with a viewport rectangle. 7 Footer hint covers only navigation. Doesn't tell you about pinch zoom, scroll-to-zoom, click-to-tune. New users have no discoverability for the actual interaction model — and that interaction model has the zoom-aware click-to-tune bug described in the research notes.
