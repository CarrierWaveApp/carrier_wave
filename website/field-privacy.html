Privacy Policy

CW Field Guide — Last updated: September 10, 2026

What CW Field Guide Does

CW Field Guide is an offline reference app for amateur radio operators. It bundles curated equipment guides, manuals, and outing checklists so they are available in the field without an internet connection.

Data We Collect

None. CW Field Guide does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your reading position, bookmarks, and checklist state are stored locally on your device only. They are never transmitted anywhere and are removed when you delete the app.

Network Access

All reference content ships inside the app. CW Field Guide makes no network requests at all — it works entirely offline.

Children

CW Field Guide does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Reach out on the Carrier Wave Discord or email privacy@carrierwave.app.
