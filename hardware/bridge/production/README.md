# JLCPCB assembly files — CW Bridge

Generated by `../jlcpcb_export.py` from `cw_bridge.kicad_sch` (LCSC fields) +
`cw_bridge.kicad_pcb` (placements). Regenerate after any schematic/PCB change:

```bash
python3 ../jlcpcb_export.py
```

- `cw_bridge_bom.csv` — upload as "BOM file" (Comment / Designator / Footprint / LCSC Part #)
- `cw_bridge_cpl.csv` — upload as "CPL / Pick & Place file" (Mid X/Y in mm, Y-up, Top side only)
- `cw_bridge_hand_assembly.csv` — **do NOT upload.** Reference list of the parts
  JLC does not place (no LCSC / DNP). The BOM and CPL cover the same designator
  set so JLC's DFM check ("designator doesn't exist in the CPL file") passes;
  the hand-assembled parts are tracked here instead.

LCSC part numbers were verified 2026-06-11 against JLCPCB's catalog
(via the jlcparts mirror of JLC's parts API + indexed lcsc.com/jlcpcb.com part
pages). Stock moves — re-check live stock in the JLC BOM tool at order time.

## ⚠️ Before ordering

1. **Routing is done** (re-done 2026-09-26 after the WROOM module swap and
   the panel-header rework; DRC at the 4-hole baseline, 0 unconnected).
   `cw_bridge_gerbers.zip` and `gerbers/` were exported 2026-09-28 (KiCad
   10.0.6, `kicad-cli pcb export gerbers --layers F.Cu,In1.Cu,In2.Cu,B.Cu,F.Paste,B.Paste,F.Silkscreen,B.Silkscreen,F.Mask,B.Mask,Edge.Cuts`
   + `export drill --format excellon --generate-map --map-format gerberx2`,
   then `zip -r cw_bridge_gerbers.zip gerbers`) from the **rev 2.1** board
   (JST PH panel headers, encoder on J15) — re-export if the board changes.
2. **Rotation check (mandatory):** in JLCPCB's component-placement preview,
   visually confirm pin-1/polarity of **U3 U4 U5 U7 U8 U9 U11, D3 D4 D5,
   D6–D10 (status LEDs), J1, J9, SW2, SW3, L2, L3**. (The panel headers and
   the encoder are hand-soldered JST PH housings since rev 2.1 and no longer
   appear in the preview; pin 1 = the square pad, keyed housing.)
   The CPL applies community rotation offsets (see `ROTATION_OVERRIDES` in the
   exporter), but **SOT-583 (U7), the GCT USB-C (J9), PCM12 (SW2) and the C&K
   JS202011 (SW3) have no community data** — expect to rotate those in the
   preview. SW3's pins 1/4 (RUN throw) must sit at the end nearest the `RUN`
   silk label — check against the silk in the render. D3/D5 share the
   `D_SMA` footprint where pad 1 = cathode; confirm the band in the render.
3. **D1 polarity note from the schematic docs:** the SB140 (D1) reuses a
   bidirectional cached symbol — if D1 ever returns to the BOM, its
   orientation is enforced only by footprint convention. (Not in the current
   BOM — kept here so the warning isn't lost.)

## Panel wiring (2026-09-26)

Off-board parts you supply, not JLC: a 128×64 I²C OLED (SSD1306/SH1106 class,
4-pin GND/VCC/SCL/SDA → J3), three panel LEDs with their anodes on J12.1 and
cathodes on J12.2/3/4 (series resistors are on the board), momentary
buttons for J13 RESET and J14 BOOT (plus BTN1–3 on J4–J6 as before), a
KY-040-class rotary encoder module on J15 (CLK DT SW + GND, same order as the
module's header), JST PH mating housings/leads for all of them, and the
antenna: a U.FL (IPEX MHF1) plug to SMA-female **bulkhead** pigtail from the
socket on top of the WROOM-1U module to the case wall, plus a 2.4 GHz SMA-male
antenna (SMA, not RP-SMA; MHF1, not MHF4). Nothing antenna-related is on the PCB.
